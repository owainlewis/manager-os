# Daily briefing

## When to use
A routine every weekday at 8am. Run it by hand if I ask "what did I miss?"

## Why
Most of what fills a manager's day happened while they weren't looking. This reads it all so I don't have to, and shows me only what needs me.

## Steps
1. Read my email and Slack from the last 24 hours (since Friday on a Monday).
2. Check every open project on the board: what's blocked, late or at risk.
3. Flag what needs me, in three groups:
   - **Urgent:** needs my attention now. Something is blocked on me, or someone senior is waiting.
   - **Reply today:** needs a response from me today.
   - **This week:** needs a response or decision from me this week.
4. For each item: one line on what it is and what I need to do, with a link.

## Output
A comment on today's briefing task, or a reply in our conversation if Linear isn't connected. One screen at most. If nothing needs me, say so.

## Rules
- Link every item to its source.
- Leave out anything that doesn't need me.
- Don't invent deadlines. Use the date in the message or none.
