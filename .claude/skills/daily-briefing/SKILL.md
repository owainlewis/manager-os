# Daily briefing

## When to use
A routine every weekday at 8am.

## Reads
- Today's calendar
- Email and Slack since 6pm yesterday
- Linear: tasks blocked or due today
- Notion: Me, Goals and the Team pages of anyone I meet today

## Steps
1. List today's meetings in order. For each, say what I need to prepare and link anything to read first. For a 1:1, link the person's Notion page.
2. List requests waiting on me from email and Slack, oldest first, with who asked and a link.
3. List Linear tasks that are blocked or due today.
4. Name the one thing that most needs my attention today, and why, in one sentence.

## Output
A comment on today's briefing task. One screen at most.

## Rules
- Skip a section if it's empty.
- Link every item to its source.
- Don't invent context about a meeting. If the invite has no agenda, say so.
