# Meeting prep

## When to use
Before any meeting that isn't a 1:1. As a routine, it arrives the morning of the meeting.

## Reads
- The calendar invite and attached docs
- The transcript of the last meeting with the same people
- Related email, Slack and Linear issues

## Steps
1. Say what the meeting is for in one sentence, from the invite. If it doesn't say, write "purpose not stated" and suggest asking the organiser.
2. List who's attending and what each person last said about this topic, quoted, with links.
3. List open actions from the last meeting and whether each is done, checked against Linear.
4. List what I need to decide or bring.
5. Suggest up to three questions.

## Output
A comment on the meeting's task, or a section in the daily briefing.

## Rules
- One screen at most.
- Quote people rather than summarise what they think.
