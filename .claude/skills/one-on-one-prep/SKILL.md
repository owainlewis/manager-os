# 1:1 prep

## When to use
The morning of every 1:1.

## Reads
- The person's page under Team in Notion
- My Slack and email with them since our last 1:1
- Their Linear tasks
- The transcript of our last 1:1 in Zoom

## Steps
1. List what I promised and haven't done.
2. List questions they asked that I didn't answer.
3. List signals worth asking about: a task stuck over a week, fewer messages or contributions, a change in tone. Link each one.
4. Remind me what they said they're working toward.
5. Suggest three questions, at least one about their career.

## Output
A comment on the 1:1 task.

## Rules
- A signal is a reason to ask, not a judgement.
- Never rate performance or count activity.
- Leave out anything I wouldn't say to their face.
