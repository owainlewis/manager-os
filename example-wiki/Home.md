# Alpha Corporation: Engineering wiki

This is the wiki for the Payments Engineering group at Alpha Corporation, a made-up company for the AI Manager OS course. Everything here is fictional.

## What lives where

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Tasks and projects | Linear (team PAY) |
| Notes about me, our goals, my team and our decisions | This wiki |
| Meeting notes and transcripts | Zoom. Link to them, never copy them here |
| Conversations | Slack and email |
| Code | GitHub (alpha-corp/payments) |

## Pages

- [Me](Me.md): my role, what I own and how I work
- [Goals](Goals.md): what we're trying to achieve this quarter
- [Team](Team.md): one page per direct report
- [Stakeholders](Stakeholders.md): the people outside the team I work with most
- [How we work](How%20we%20work.md): our meetings, reviews and rules
- [Decisions](Decisions.md): one line per decision, newest first

## Rules for this wiki

- Add a page only if the agent will need it more than once, and it lives nowhere else.
- Delete a line when it stops being true. Out-of-date notes cost more than missing ones.
- Write only what you'd say to the person's face.
