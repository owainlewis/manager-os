# What we own

The services the Payments Engineering group owns, who leads each one, and who outside the team cares most. Work for each lives in Linear (team ALP).

| Service | What it does | Lead | Who cares most |
|---|---|---|---|
| Payouts | Sends money from Alpha to our business customers' bank accounts, through our bank partner. | Sam Okafor (Payouts v2), with Tom Reyes | Ben Carter, Northwind Freight |
| Payments API | The public API customers use to create and track payouts. | Elena Costa | Customers' engineering teams |
| Business onboarding (KYB) | Checks a new business customer and their documents before their first payout. | Hannah Lee (Onboarding team, 5 engineers) | Greg Holt |
| Reconciliation and reporting | Matches every payout against the bank's settlement file and produces month-end reports for Finance. | Daniel Brooks | Maya Singh |
| Platform | CI, deploys and shared libraries for the group. Runs the code review agent pilot. | Priya Nair | The whole group |
| Testing and release | The regression suite and release sign-off. QA signs off any change to payouts or money movement. | Aisha Rahman | Rachel Stone |
